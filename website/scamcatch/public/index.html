It talks like someone who’s on your side.

Getting scammed isn’t a failure of intelligence. So the voice doesn’t lecture. It listens, asks questions, and helps you reach the answer yourself.

  • It listens before it warns.

    That sounds like someone who means a lot to you. How did you two meet?

  • It gives you a reason to check, not a verdict.

    Before anything moves, let’s look up that platform together.

  • You stay in charge.

    You decide who else hears about this, if anyone.
