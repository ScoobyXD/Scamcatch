1. Publish only the public directory: index.html, styles.css, app.js, vision.js, live.js, logo.svg, favicon.svg, and fonts/. The site needs no build step or package install.
2. Serve index.html for the root path. Serve app.js, vision.js, and live.js as text/javascript, styles.css as text/css, fonts/*.woff2 as font/woff2, and logo.svg and favicon.svg as image/svg+xml.
3. Fonts are self-hosted Newsreader and Hanken Grotesk variable fonts from @fontsource-variable (SIL Open Font License). Do not load fonts from Google.
4. The page has six snap-scrolling stages and a right-side section index: Live demo, The vision, How it works, The voice, Where it stands, Try it.
5. The live demo plays a scam chat, a ScamCatch warning, and a "Tap here to talk" voice screen, then replays after 16 seconds without interaction. Tapping opens the call screen. The "Pick a scam to watch" buttons under the progress bar switch between eight scenarios (romance: Simon offers to match $40,000 in a Monero fund, bank login favor for $100,000, fake Amazon fraud department, tech support, IRS, jury duty, lottery/Publishers Clearing House, fake Brad Pitt asking for $20,000, with stand-in silhouette photos rather than real photos of him). The scenario text lives in SCENARIOS in public/app.js; ?scam=<id> opens on one (for example scamcatch.io/?scam=irs). The live voice gets whichever chat is on screen.
6. live.js defines window.ScamCatchLive.start(context, { onStatus, onCaption }), which returns a session with end(). Without live.js, the call screen says live voice isn't connected.
7. The live voice context is the chat shown in the demo. ScamCatchDemo.context() returns it.
8. The demo chats use three looks, set per scam by "app" in SCENARIOS (public/app.js): romance is a dark WhatsApp-style chat, bank favor and celebrity are a dark Messenger-style chat, and the rest are iMessage-style. They are styled after those apps only. Do not use Apple, iMessage, WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, or Meta logos. The romance contact is named Simon. Unknown senders use fictional 555-01xx numbers. Real company and celebrity names appear only as the scammer's claim, never with their logos or photos.
9. The vision stage shows ScamCatch on every adult's phone as one frontline layer of many, not a silver bullet. The map is the lower 48 from us-atlas 3 states-10m (U.S. Census Bureau, ISC license), projected with d3-geo geoAlbers into a 960x610 viewBox and inlined in index.html. COAST and METROS in vision.js use that projection. vision.js draws about 34 people holding smartphones on their left; scam messages fly in from overseas and turn a phone red, then the ScamCatch logo pops up over that phone and pulls the scam into it. A card follows one person at a time, using the flagged message and the first sentence of the alert from SCENARIOS in app.js (exposed as ScamCatchDemo.scenarios), with the ScamCatch logo beside the alert. Three icons near the geographic center of the lower 48 (HUBS in vision.js) stand for Bank, Authorities (an original eagle head in an olive wreath, not any agency's seal; the card's SVG path is the one shape, and vision.js draws it on the map too), and Trusted contacts. No person is placed in the zone around them (HUB_ZONE). Each trace ends at the edge of its icon, and its card row shows Sent on that same frame. Only the card person sends report traces to them, one at a time, and each row in the card (Your bank, The authorities, Your trusted contacts) appears with Sent as its trace lands. Other catches only make the icons glow softly, to keep the map readable. A new scam starts elsewhere about every 0.9 to 1.5 seconds. The card says reports go out with the person's OK; keep that wording. The cities, people, and attempts are illustrative, not live data. It animates only while on screen and shows a still frame with reduced motion.
10. The website has no contact form, email address, analytics, or advertising scripts.
11. Start Website.cmd runs server.py (Python 3.11+, standard library only). It serves public/ at http://localhost:5180 with Cache-Control: no-store and adds POST /api/voice/session.
12. The endpoint takes {"sdp", "context"} and returns {"sdp", "greeting", "noBackendCommentary"}, or {"error"} with a short message. context is ScamCatchDemo.context().
13. Before voice works, fill in voiceKeySecret.secretId and voiceKeySecret.region in infra/deployment.json. jsonField is optional. The server window says what is missing.
14. Voice prompts, key handling, and cost limits are in docs/voice.md under Website voice rules.
15. On scamcatch.io, /api/* goes through CloudFront to the scamcatch-voice Lambda (infra/voice/handler.py), which reads the same secret and mirrors server.py. It allows 50 calls per UTC day and one new call every 10 seconds, counted in the scamcatch-voice-usage DynamoDB table. Deploy Voice.cmd in the repository root builds and deploys it with infra/deploy_voice.py, then checks the endpoint without starting a call. Results go to infra/voice-deploy-log.txt. Run it again after editing AIInstructions.kt or handler.py.
